[[def: perennial, perennials, Perennial]]

~ A perennial is a plant that lives for more than two years. Unlike annuals, which complete their life cycle in one season, perennials return year after year from their root system or other persistent structures.

~ Perennials are valued in gardens for their longevity and reduced need for replanting. Many fruit trees, berry bushes, and ornamental plants are perennials. In [[ref: Greenhouse]] settings, tropical perennials can be cultivated in temperate climates. Related terms: [[ref: Pruning]], [[ref: Harvest]].
