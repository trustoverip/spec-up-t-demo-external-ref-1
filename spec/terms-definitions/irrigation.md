[[def: irrigation, irrigating, Irrigation]]

~ Irrigation is the controlled application of water for agricultural purposes through artificial means. It involves the systematic delivery of water to plants via channels, pipes, sprinklers, drip systems, or other distribution methods to ensure optimal plant growth.

~ Proper irrigation management considers soil type, plant water requirements, evapotranspiration rates, and water conservation. In [[ref: Greenhouse]] environments, irrigation is often automated and precisely controlled. Related terms: [[ref: Soil]], [[ref: Watering]], [[ref: Harvest]].
